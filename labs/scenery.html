Eight candidates, built rather than described. The scene today is all weather and no world: everything in it crosses and nothing stays, so there is no reference for the motion. The split that matters is not which idea is prettiest, it is drifter against fixture: a drifter drops into Ambient.KINDS as a config entry and a markup function, a fixture needs somewhere to live that is not the spawner. Fixtures are the better answer and the more expensive one.
